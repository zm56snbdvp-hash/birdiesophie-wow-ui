# Birdie Command Deck v0.3

## Design decision

The second supplied concept is the implementation baseline because it preserves more world visibility and keeps the eye path compact. The painted Teldrassil clubhouse is mood reference only; the addon does not fake or replace the game world.

## Live MVP

- Centered Combat Zone remains inside 46.5% of the logical width.
- Player, target, focus, castbars and primary action bars form one bottom-center eye path.
- Clubhouse Comms and Caddie Scorecard remain peripheral and visually recede outside combat.
- Thin champagne scorecard rules, forest-tinted surfaces and warm cream text create the brand without ornamental clutter.
- Current Druid form receives one restrained accent color.
- Crowd control on Birdietee receives the only large central warning.
- Faerie Fire, Entangling Roots, Hibernate and Cyclone appear on the target scorecard when present.
- Danger red is limited to control loss and player health at or below 30%.

## Deliberately deferred

- Exact Details! anchoring remains manual until its live frame identity is confirmed.
- Full cooldown and enemy-interrupt intelligence belongs in the next WeakAura package.
- Imported fonts and bitmap ornaments are deferred to avoid client-path and redistribution fragility.
- No reference screenshot, chat content, SavedVariables or account identifiers enter the public repository.
